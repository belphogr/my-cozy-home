# Third-party 3D assets

The following Sketchfab assets are included as optimized GLB derivatives and retain their original attribution requirements.

- **Globe on a wooden stand in Victorian style** by **risteralline** — [Sketchfab source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/globe-on-a-wooden-stand-in-victorian-style-54245a6f6f8a425d93dfa3892445cab4) — licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Texture resolution was reduced for web delivery and the model was centered and scaled.
- **Modern Generic Smartphone** by **Ottto3ds** — [Sketchfab source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/modern-generic-smartphone-cb96f6c19c6c416f8f52a127dd6ec05b) — licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Texture resolution was reduced and the model was positioned on a stand.
- **Ikea Bergenes** by **proAV** — [Sketchfab source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ikea-bergenes-387a804ded3549a7a1bdca37ab492078) — licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Texture resolution was reduced and the model was combined with the smartphone for web delivery.
- **Shiba** by **zixisun02 / zixisun51** — [Sketchfab source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/shiba-faef9fe5ace445e7b2989d1c1ece361c) — licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The static source was rigged and given original resting and walking animation loops for this project.
- **Catpuccino!** by **Samantha “Siyrex” Johns** — [Sketchfab source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/catpuccino-f264bf3effb3443db45217225a5bab8f) — licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Textures were reduced for web delivery and the model was scaled for the living-room coffee table.
